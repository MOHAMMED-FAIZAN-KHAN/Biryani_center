The Nawabs Legacy

Founded by Nawas Ali Khan in the heart of Hyderabad, our restaurant has been the guardian of authentic Dum Biryani recipes for over three decades. What started as a small family kitchen is now a celebrated culinary destination.

We still use the same traditional methods - slow cooking in sealed handis, premium aged basmati rice, and the perfect blend of 17 spices that make our biryani legendary.
